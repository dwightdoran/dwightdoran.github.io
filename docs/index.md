# Dwight Doran

I'm a software engineer and Army veteran studying cybersecurity through SANS. My interests include incident response and industrial control systems security.

This portfolio follows my learning through CTF participation, technical investigation, and reflection on the evidence behind my conclusions.

## How I became interested in cybersecurity

A few years ago, while working as a software engineer, I picked up a backlog ticket for a potential SQL injection vulnerability identified by a security scan. That part of the Ruby backend used raw SQL rather than Active Record, its object-relational mapper. I fixed the issue, then found myself wanting to look for other vulnerabilities in the codebase. What began as one ticket became a lasting interest in understanding how software can fail and how to make it more secure.

## My CTF experience

Fall 2026 NCL was my first CTF. I was nervous, especially about the team portion, but the Gym exercises helped me realize I was more prepared than I had initially thought. I enjoyed the experience and came away wishing I had entered the Spring 2026 competition too.

A highlight was completing all the Web App Exploitation questions for my team. We divided the work according to our strengths, and I took on a section my teammates felt less comfortable with or had not attempted in the Individual Game.
